How you show up

Be where students already plan their future.

One partnership, every province. You do not need to be national to take part: a local employer is exactly what a student searching near their own school is hoping to find. The term runs from one spring launch to the next, and your content does not wait for the event.

First to go live

LaunchPad

Your clips go live on LaunchPad first, ahead of the spring and November dates. LaunchPad is myBlueprint’s career video hub: built into the platform students already use, and open to anyone with the link, no account needed. So it works in your own newsletter or careers page too.

Partners come in through the year. Your clips go live on LaunchPad first, your sector page and directory entry follow, and you take part in the November launch alongside everyone else. We confirm your go-live date when you sign.

The term runs to the next spring launch whenever you join, so a later start makes for a shorter first year. Join after the November event and you begin on the following term, with your content live in the meantime.

Associations. If you represent members and want them on the record rather than on camera, that is a different conversation and we should have it. Directory coverage is scoped province by province.

Post-secondary partners join as content partners: career storytelling, not admissions marketing. Nothing on a student-facing surface asks a student for anything: no form, no application, no email capture, no account.
